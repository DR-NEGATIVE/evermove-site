Evermove Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Evermove collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and the app has no internet access.

What the app stores

To run your plan, Evermove saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your settings: training level, age (used to estimate heart-rate zones), weight unit and whether voice coaching is on.
  • Your activity: which sessions you finished and when, the weights and reps you logged, walking check-ins and balance test times.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. We have no servers and no accounts, and the Android app does not request internet permission.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, account, name, email or contact details.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health-connect data.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Voice coaching

Spoken cues use your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. Evermove only passes it short exercise prompts such as "Rest, 60 seconds". It never passes anything you typed.

Copying and deleting your data

Library → Copy data copies your data to the clipboard as text, and only when you tap it. What you do with it after that is up to you.

Library → Reset erases your plan and progress. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Evermove is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Evermove offers general exercise guidance. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition.
